only one program can control a specific port at any given time.

Think of your computer's IP address (127.0.0.1) as the street address of a massive apartment building.
The ports are the individual apartment room numbers.Mail (network traffic) sent to Room 8000 was being signed for by the old tenant who didn't know your code.
Moving your application to Room 8005 meant your specific application was finally the one opening the door and handling the incoming requests.


To get port no:On Windows :
cmdnetstat -ano | findstr LISTENING

Look for a line showing 0.0.0.0:8000 or similar numbers at the end of the IP thats your port number
